## VERIFICATION AND QUALITY STANDARDS

### Fact Verification Process

1. **Cross-reference**: Information checked against multiple institutional sources
2. **Currency**: Recommendations verified as current (2024-2026 practices)
3. **Regional Appropriateness**: Information assessed for Indian agricultural context
4. **Farmer Applicability**: Recommendations practical for farmer implementation

### Fertilizer Dosage Verification

**Standard Applied**: 
- No fertilizer dosage stated without institutional source
- All doses marked as "general ranges" or "verify with soil test"
- Specific dosages attributed to crop, region, soil test results

### Pesticide Information Verification

**Standard Applied**:
- No pesticide names, doses, or mixing ratios stated without source
- All chemical control recommendations marked as "consult local expert"
- IPM emphasis throughout

## LIMITATION AND DISCLAIMER

### Information Scope

This knowledge base represents:
- General agricultural principles applicable to India
- Broad guidance suitable for diverse regions
- Foundation knowledge for RAG integration

This knowledge base does NOT replace:
- Soil testing and local soil assessment
- Consultation with local agricultural experts
- Extension officer guidance for specific situations
- Professional diagnosis for unusual problems

### Regional Variations

Agricultural recommendations vary significantly by:
- State and district (soil, climate, rainfall)
- Specific field conditions (soil test, topography)
- Farmer resources and capabilities
- Market demand and pricing
- Crop variety and age

This knowledge base provides framework; local expertise applies specifics.
